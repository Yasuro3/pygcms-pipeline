# AI assistance for the v1.4.0 build

ChatGPT (OpenAI) assisted with implementing the new QC/reporting engine, adapters,
CLI, tests and documentation, and with checking saved analysis outputs. The actual
executed checks and unresolved validation limits are recorded in VALIDATION_REPORT.md.
Synthetic fixtures are labelled as synthetic and were not represented as experiments.
The developer/author remains responsible for reviewing the source, chemical rules,
outputs and suitability for each application before public release or publication.

The v1.3.2 AI disclosure is retained separately as inherited historical documentation;
it is not evidence that its named services/models were used for this new build.
The new offline QC pass itself makes no external AI call. Optional external-model
review in the older chromatographic workflow remains separate and user-controlled.
